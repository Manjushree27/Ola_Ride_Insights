# Business Insights

## Dataset-Level Findings

- The dataset contains 103,024 bookings for July 2024.
- Successful rides account for 63,967 bookings.
- Driver cancellations are higher than customer cancellations.
- Driver-not-found cases are a meaningful operational concern.
- Raw booking value is approximately Rs 56.53M.

## Suggested Deep-Dive Questions

1. Which vehicle type has the best success rate?
2. Which vehicle type contributes the most successful revenue?
3. Which pickup locations show the highest demand?
4. Which locations have frequent driver-not-found cases?
5. What are the most common driver cancellation reasons?
6. What are the most common customer cancellation reasons?
7. Do ratings differ across vehicle types?
8. Are longer rides associated with higher booking values?

## Executive Recommendations

- Improve supply planning in top pickup zones.
- Build a driver cancellation monitoring report.
- Reduce customer wait-time issues by tracking `V_TAT` and `C_TAT`.
- Use high-performing vehicle categories for promotional campaigns.
- Track cancellation rate as a daily operational KPI.
