# Power BI Dashboard Design

## Page 1: Overview
- KPI cards: total bookings, successful bookings, success rate, total booking value, average ride distance.
- Charts: booking status distribution, daily booking trend, vehicle type split.
- Slicers: date, booking status, vehicle type, payment method, pickup location.

## Page 2: Revenue
- Revenue by vehicle type.
- Average booking value by vehicle type.
- Payment method contribution.
- Daily booking value trend.

## Page 3: Customer
- Top pickup and drop locations.
- Customer cancellation reasons.
- Customer rating by vehicle type.
- Repeat customer analysis if customer-level aggregation is required.

## Page 4: Operations
- Vehicle turnaround time and customer turnaround time.
- Ride distance distribution.
- Driver-not-found cases by pickup location.
- Operational failure rate by vehicle type.

## Page 5: Ratings
- Driver rating and customer rating by vehicle type.
- Rating distribution.
- Low-rated segments needing service improvement.

## Page 6: Cancellation
- Canceled by customer vs canceled by driver.
- Top cancellation reasons.
- Cancellation rate by vehicle type and location.

## Page 7: Executive Summary
- Five key findings.
- Three business recommendations.
- Risks and limitations.
- Suggested next steps.
